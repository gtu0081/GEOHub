# Commercial Licensing

Status: `inquiry_only`

Organizations that want terms outside `AGPL-3.0-only` may contact the copyright holder to discuss a commercial license. An inquiry creates no offer, entitlement, or license. Availability, scope, pricing, support, and any exception require a separate signed agreement.

Contact the GitHub maintainer identified for this repository or open a repository Issue with the `commercial-licensing` label. Include no confidential information, customer data, credentials, or proposed contract terms in a public Issue. The maintainer may provide a private contact channel after initial triage.

No commercial license is granted by this document.
